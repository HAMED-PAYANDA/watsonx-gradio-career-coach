# 🚀 AI Career Coach: Your Personalized Job Application Assistant

**Estimated completion time:** 60 minutes

Welcome to the AI Career Coach lab! This project is a hands-on journey into mastering the integration of cutting-edge large language models (LLMs) into user-friendly web applications. 

By the end of this project, you will move beyond the basics of prompt engineering to develop three specialized, production-ready tools:
*   📄 **AI-Driven Resume Enhancer:** Tailor and optimize resumes for specific job descriptions.
*   ✍️ **Personalized Cover Letter Generator:** Draft customized, compelling cover letters in seconds.
*   🧭 **Strategic Job Hunting Advisor:** Provide personalized career improvement advice based on AI analysis.

"AI Career Coach" is more than just a learning experience; it's a stepping stone to a future where artificial intelligence bridges the gap between talent and opportunity. 

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
Upon completing this project, you will be able to:
*   **Build with Gradio:** Gain proficiency in rapidly building and deploying AI-based web interfaces.
*   **Integrate watsonx.ai:** Harness the power of enterprise-grade large language models for real-world applications.
*   **Apply Practical AI Solutions:** Understand how to apply generative AI to solve highly specific, practical problems in the modern job market.
